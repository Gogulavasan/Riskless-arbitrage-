CIRP CALCULATOR

Enter a pair, tenor, spot, market forward and both risk-free rates. The engine computes the covered-interest-parity forward, the mispricing, the arbitrage direction, then runs the round trip through itemised real-world costs and two tax regimes. Covered parity only — no exchange-rate risk is taken.

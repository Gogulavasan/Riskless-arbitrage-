Get alerts when this page is closed

Browser alerts only work while the tab is open. For alerts on your phone, the repository includes a checker that runs every 15 minutes on GitHub and messages a Telegram chat when an opportunity appears. Setup takes about five minutes:

  1. In Telegram, create a bot with @BotFather and copy its token; send your bot one message, then read your chat id from https://api.telegram.org/bot<TOKEN>/getUpdates.
  2. In the GitHub repo: Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → add TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID.
  3. Put your real forward points in monitor_config.json and set quotes_are_real to true. The checker uses the same file as this page.

How it works

1 · Live spotThe current exchange rate is pulled from a public feed every minute.
2 · Fair forwardFrom spot and the two interest rates, the rate at which a forward should trade.
3 · Your quoteYour broker's forward points are added to spot to get the market forward.
4 · Gap and costsMarket minus fair is the gap; spreads, funding and fees are taken off.
5 · AlertIf what is left is above your threshold, you get a banner, a sound and a browser notification.

Forward quotes are the one input no free feed provides, so the page ships with sample values and stays in "sample" mode until you enter real quotes. In major pairs a genuine gap after costs is rare and small; a large one usually means a stale quote or a currency with capital controls. Not investment advice.

ARBITRAGE INSIGHTS

An FX Arbitrage Intelligence Engine. It detects Covered Interest Rate Parity deviations between currency pairs, decides which currency to borrow and which to invest, runs the trade through real bid-ask, funding, margin and fee frictions, then applies a country tax layer — so the answer is not "is there an arbitrage" but "what is left after the real world".

How the engine works

Ingestspot, market forward, both risk-free rates, tenor, day-count basis per currency
Implythe CIRP forward from spot and the two rates
Compareimplied vs market: mispricing in price units and annualised bp; premium/discount both ways
Directforward too dear → borrow quote, sell base forward; too cheap → borrow base, buy base forward
Costeach friction switched on in turn, on the correct side of every quote
Taxinterest earned, interest paid and the FX result taxed as each country actually treats them
Sanitythe higher-rate currency must trade at a forward discount; gaps above 25 bp are suspect data

Built for CFA Level 1 study. Covered parity only — never Uncovered IRP. Tax rates are placeholders; verify before use.

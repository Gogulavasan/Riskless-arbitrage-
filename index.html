ARBITRAGE INSIGHTS

A simple FX arbitrage tool. It compares the fair forward rate of a currency pair with the market forward rate, finds the gap, works out which currency to borrow and which to invest in, then shows what is left after real-world costs and tax — so the answer is not "is there an arbitrage" but "what is left after the real world".

What is FX arbitrage? Borrow one currency, invest in the other, lock the exchange rate today. The opportunityIf the locked-in forward rate differs from the fair rate, there is a gain with no currency risk. The reality checkBid-ask spreads, funding costs, fees and tax decide whether that gain survives. The resultGross gain → after costs → after tax.

How the engine works

Inputsspot rate, market forward rate, the two interest rates, number of days
Fair ratework out the fair forward rate from spot and the two interest rates
Comparefair forward vs market forward — the gap is the opportunity
Tradeborrow one currency, invest in the other, lock the exchange rate with a forward
Costsspreads, funding, margin and fees, taken off one by one
Taxthe gain taxed the way each country treats it
Checka gap that looks too good usually means bad data, not free money

The exchange rate is locked in advance, so there is no currency risk. Tax rates are placeholders; verify before use.
